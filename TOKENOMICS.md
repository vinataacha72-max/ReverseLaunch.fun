# LaunchOn Reverse ($REVERSE) — Tokenomics Overview

**Domain:** launchonReverse.fun  
**X:** [@LaunchOnRVE](https://x.com/LaunchOnRVE)

## Core Philosophy
LaunchOn Reverse flips the traditional launchpad order:
**Buyers first → Coin second → Dev last.**

This removes the structural advantage that snipers, bundlers, and insiders currently enjoy on most platforms.

---

## Revenue Split (per fee claim)

| Portion | % | Destination | Purpose |
|---------|---|-------------|---------|
| Creators / Queue | **70%** | Token creator or original queue participants (optional X-handle routing) | Incentivize quality launches & reward early fair participants |
| Buy & Burn | **20%** | Market buy $REVERSE → permanent burn | Continuous deflationary pressure |
| Treasury | **10%** | Protocol treasury | Development, audits, liquidity, grants |

All claims and burns are executed on-chain and publicly verifiable.

## Key Design Principles

1. **Non-custodial** — Users always control their keys and funds
2. **Anti-snipe by design** — No contract address until the queue closes
3. **Transparent flywheel** — Every fee claim is visible on-chain
4. **Dual-chain native** — One experience across Solana and Robinhood Chain
5. **No hidden team dumps** — Clear vesting / no unfair unlocks
